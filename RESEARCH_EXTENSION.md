# Research extension: acceptance and evidence

This extends the pilot; it does not replace the original M3/M5 scientific gates.

1. **Independent references (M7).** Obtain 10–20 visually selected, distributed stable landmarks per scorable pilot pair. Bind annotations to exact source image hashes and common-grid bounds, retain per-image picking uncertainties and reviewer provenance, and quantify second-review disagreement where available. Mark genuinely unscorable pairs explicitly. The blank zoomable packet is `reports/landmark_review.html`; generate using `landmark_review.py`, import human review JSON with `import_review.py`. No generated or matcher-proposed coordinates can establish this acceptance. No real-pair reference is independently validated yet.
2. **Expanded benchmark and original CNSFM (M8).** `expanded_solar_manifest.json` contains all 42 published CNSFM observations and 321 unordered pairs (45 S1, 45 S2, 231 S3). The original manifest and pilot measurements stay frozen. The Windows workflow executes the original pinned author binary and published crater detections, first in probe mode. Raw output parsing and conversion must be audited before common-protocol scoring. Native-resolution author inputs differ from the 512px baseline grid; a common-grid experiment is additionally required before direct performance claims. Add geographically disjoint Tycho data from MoonAnything, keeping synthetic and real evidence separate. Further genuinely new real repeat-observation regions remain required.
3. **Failure rejection (M9).** `evaluate_extended.py` records forward/reverse cycle error, supporting-point coverage, scale, rotation and translation plausibility. `confidence.py` selects policies only from independently labelled development rows, with explicit incorrect-acceptance counts and the full retention trade-off. Map-reference disagreement is never a correctness label. An uncertainty band leaves ambiguous landmark errors unresolved. Held-out performance must be reported after freezing the policy. S3 was inspected during the pilot, so new choices evaluated there are exploratory.
4. **Actual illumination (M10).** `solar_metadata.py` archives source pages and derives crop-local geometric Sun azimuth/elevation from LROC subsolar coordinates. Source metadata precision and assumptions are explicit; image north azimuth is never relabelled as solar direction. Bright overlap on real provisional grids is a labelled intensity proxy, not a shadow ground truth. Physical MoonAnything render pairs provide known-geometry changing-light controls. Report Sun-angle strata, aligned common non-dark support, and failures separately from gain/gamma software controls.
5. **Lunar adaptation (M11).** Acquire checksummed MoonAnything physical renders and metadata, freeze geographic and source-observation-disjoint train/dev/test subsets before outcomes, train a limited adaptation on training data, choose a checkpoint using development data only, then compare frozen and adapted matching on unseen geographic data. Evaluate transfer to real independently labelled lunar observations; synthetic gains alone do not establish transfer. Preserve negative results and trained-model provenance.

## Current execution

- Extended bidirectional evaluation: `runs/expanded-confidence-v1` completed all 1,926 measurements. Saved geometry and source hashes have been independently recomputed by `verify_extension.py`; map-reference uncertainty remains unresolved.
- Physical source sample: Zenodo record 18415119; `metadata.zip` and `multilighting_hapke_rendering.zip` retrieved through exact byte ranges from the outer ZIP, verified by decompression CRC32 and saved SHA256. No full-archive MD5 claim.
- Original CNSFM: all 642 common-grid Windows executions completed and were audited for exact inputs, output hashes, coordinate IDs and exhaustive pair/direction coverage. Shared-geometry scoring returned 45/45 S1, 42/45 S2 and 115/231 S3 fits. These are returned-fit counts, not independently verified correctness. Native author input probes also completed in all three scenes. Whole-image crater redetection differs from the native tiled pipeline.
- Physical adaptation: 80 acquisition-separated Tycho patches frozen before outcomes; 300-step descriptor projection selected on development data. Held-out correct fits were 98/128 versus 93/128 frozen. A corrected exploratory 95% interval joins overlapping footprints and shared acquisitions into 15 components: −2.50 to +10.42 percentage points (the earlier 16-source-only grouping was −2.34 to +10.94). The adapter policy accepted three incorrect held-out fits. See `reports/EXTENSION_REPORT.md` for denominators, failures and limitations.
- New real observations: the separate checksummed appearance archive supplied 54 real LRO orthocrops from 32 acquisitions and 27 metadata-selected repeat pairs across five Tycho areas. Real dev/test groups and footprints are separated; physical training/development terrain and all physical source acquisitions are excluded. The unchanged adapter and frozen baselines were evaluated on all 27 pairs (108 measurements), with exact-pixel and saved-geometry audits. Map agreement varies by area; no consistent real accuracy gain is established.
- `import_review.py`, `rebase_review.py` and `score_independent.py` import actual human picks, convert them using known source-grid geometry and score the corresponding saved-prediction subset. Real policies are frozen from known development labels before independent held-out errors are computed. They explicitly report when insufficient labels prevent calibration. No independent scores have been generated without human review.
- Human reference review, real-data gate calibration and independently verified real-data adaptation transfer remain open. Executed unlabelled real experiments do not satisfy those scientific acceptance criteria. CNSFM common-grid execution and additional real-region evaluation are complete; neither is a claim of true navigation accuracy.
- The physical-lighting coverage audit is now in `physical_lighting_analysis.py`: counts include failed fits, shared support is explicitly a raw-I/F proxy, and thresholds 0/.005/.01/.02 are compared without tuning matchers. The anchor protocol's Sun separation stops below 81° and couples light index to rotation. The completed, audited all-Sun-pair follow-up fixes geometry, spans 13.52–155.64°, and evaluates 576 pairs with five unchanged methods (2,880 measurements). NCC is correct on 344 pairs, frozen learned matching on 303, and the adapter on 323. The old learned gate retains just five correct fits and accepts seven incorrect fits. These findings are preserved in `reports/CROSS_LIGHTING_REPORT.md`; no model or policy is selected from this inspected follow-up terrain.
