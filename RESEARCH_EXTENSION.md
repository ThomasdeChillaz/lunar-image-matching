# Research extension: acceptance and evidence

This extends the pilot; it does not replace the original M3/M5 scientific gates.

1. **Independent references (M7).** Obtain 10–20 visually selected, distributed stable landmarks per scorable pilot pair. Bind annotations to exact source image hashes and common-grid bounds, retain per-image picking uncertainties and reviewer provenance, and quantify second-review disagreement where available. Mark genuinely unscorable pairs explicitly. The blank zoomable packet is `reports/landmark_review.html`; generate using `landmark_review.py`, import human review JSON with `import_review.py`. No generated or matcher-proposed coordinates can establish this acceptance. No real-pair reference is independently validated yet.
2. **Expanded benchmark and original CNSFM (M8).** `expanded_solar_manifest.json` contains all 42 published CNSFM observations and 321 unordered pairs (45 S1, 45 S2, 231 S3). The original manifest and pilot measurements stay frozen. The Windows workflow executes the original pinned author binary and published crater detections, first in probe mode. Raw output parsing and conversion must be audited before common-protocol scoring. Native-resolution author inputs differ from the 512px baseline grid; a common-grid experiment is additionally required before direct performance claims. Add geographically disjoint Tycho data from MoonAnything, keeping synthetic and real evidence separate. Further genuinely new real repeat-observation regions remain required.
3. **Failure rejection (M9).** `evaluate_extended.py` records forward/reverse cycle error, supporting-point coverage, scale, rotation and translation plausibility. `confidence.py` selects policies only from independently labelled development rows, with explicit incorrect-acceptance counts and the full retention trade-off. Map-reference disagreement is never a correctness label. An uncertainty band leaves ambiguous landmark errors unresolved. Held-out performance must be reported after freezing the policy. S3 was inspected during the pilot, so new choices evaluated there are exploratory.
4. **Actual illumination (M10).** `solar_metadata.py` archives source pages and derives crop-local geometric Sun azimuth/elevation from LROC subsolar coordinates. Source metadata precision and assumptions are explicit; image north azimuth is never relabelled as solar direction. Bright overlap on real provisional grids is a labelled intensity proxy, not a shadow ground truth. Physical MoonAnything render pairs provide known-geometry changing-light controls. Report Sun-angle strata, aligned common non-dark support, and failures separately from gain/gamma software controls.
5. **Lunar adaptation (M11).** Acquire checksummed MoonAnything physical renders and metadata, freeze geographic and source-observation-disjoint train/dev/test subsets before outcomes, train a limited adaptation on training data, choose a checkpoint using development data only, then compare frozen and adapted matching on unseen geographic data. Evaluate transfer to real independently labelled lunar observations; synthetic gains alone do not establish transfer. Preserve negative results and trained-model provenance.
